A tiny bookmarklet that shows a separate audio lane for every Nextcloud Talk participant, tapped from incoming WebRTC tracks.

Install

Preparing bookmarklet…

  1. Show your browser’s bookmarks bar.
  2. Drag 🌊 Talk onto the bar. You can also click it to copy, then create a bookmark and paste into its URL field.
  3. Open a Nextcloud Talk call and click the bookmark before joining. Loading it later still works, but starting first gives the most reliable participant separation.
  4. Each participant gets an overlay. Click its mode button to cycle views, or use − to collapse that participant’s graph and Show audio to reopen it.
  • Live waveform
  • 15-second level history
  • Live spectrum
  • Persistent 15-second spectrogram
