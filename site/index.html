~ / talk-waveforms

See the call audio.

See what the others hear from your microphone, and whose microphone that bark or hum is coming from. Works in Nextcloud Talk, Jitsi Meet, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and other calls that run in your browser.

Install01

Preparing bookmarklet…

This is cool, but it only works on a desktop computer. Have a look anyway: press play on the clip below and tap 🌊 Talk to see it in action.

This is a bookmarklet: a bookmark that, instead of opening a site, runs a small tool on the page you are looking at. Nothing gets installed and nothing leaves your browser.

Steps for your browser

  1. Show your browser’s bookmarks bar.
  2. Drag 🌊 Talk onto the bar.

Try it now02

Press play, then click your new 🌊 Talk bookmark.

A graph of the sound appears in the bottom-left corner of this page. Click the bookmark again to close it.

In a call03

Join the call in your browser and click the bookmark. Every participant gets their own graph, right on their video:

  • Nextcloud Talk
  • Jitsi Meet
  • Google Meet
  • Microsoft Teams

Best effort: WhatsApp and other browser calls get a floating panel with whatever audio the page makes available.

Each graph has a button that switches between four views (waveform, 15-second level history, spectrum, 15-second spectrogram) and a − that collapses it.
